# Executive Insights

## Project Positioning
This project presents a management accounting case as a decision-intelligence dashboard rather than a static assignment.

## Business Problem
The case reflects a situation where sales growth does not automatically translate into stronger profitability. Cost volatility, delayed variance reporting, traditional overhead allocation, and weak responsibility ownership can distort managerial decision-making.

## Analytical Approach
The project applies four management accounting tools:

1. **Standard vs Actual Cost Analysis**
2. **Variance Analysis**
3. **Cost-Volume-Profit Analysis**
4. **Activity-Based Costing**

## Key Findings

### 1. Profitability Pressure
The case shows an overall unfavourable profit impact of Rs 6.7 million, driven mainly by material cost variance and sales price variance.

### 2. CVP Sensitivity
Profitability is sensitive to selling price, variable cost, and volume. In the original scenario table, a price cut with higher volume produces better profit than the base case, while cost increase reduces profit.

### 3. Costing Distortion
Traditional overhead allocation distorts product-level profitability. High-volume sachet packs are over-costed, while low-volume family packs are heavily under-costed.

### 4. Recommended Strategy
A phased ABC rollout combined with flexible budgeting is the strongest option because it improves costing accuracy without creating excessive organizational disruption.
